## Red, normal form and exact scope

`0xffffffff8103acc0`. 48 general pure entrances (ROL/ROR x four widths x six

kernel-coordinate special case nor a runtime opcode callback was added.

For a `w`-bit source x, `n = count & (w == 64 ? 63 : 31)` is the flag count.
The value rotates by `n mod w`, but the flags do not use that reduced count.
This distinction is necessary: a nonzero whole-word turn preserves the value
yet still updates CF. At n=0 every flag is preserved. At n=1 OF is defined;
at n>1 its undefined representative is zero. SF/ZF/AF/PF remain unchanged.
[Intel SDM Volume 2B, RCL/RCR/ROL/ROR](https://cdrdv2-public.intel.com/782151/253667-sdm-vol-2b.pdf)
specifies these two count roles and the CF/OF rules.

The bit loop disappears by substitution. For w=8 or 16, define

```text
K_w   = sum_(j=0..64/w-1) 2^(j*w)
T_w(x)= (x & (2^w-1)) * K_w
ROL_w = low_w(rotl64(T_w(x),n))
ROR_w = low_w(rotr64(T_w(x),n))
```

The terms of T occupy disjoint bit fields; their sum has no carries. Its bit
period is w. A single rotate of this periodic word followed by the low-width
projection therefore gives exactly the narrow rotate, including a whole turn.
K_8=0x0101010101010101; K_16=0x0001000100010001. Width 32/64 directly uses the
corresponding Wasm rotate. This is an exact closed value formula, not a global
minimal-work/span or faster-cycle proof. Architectural work/span counters are
not populated with invented costs.

CF is result bit 0 for ROL, result bit w-1 for ROR. At n=1, OF is the XOR of
the result's most-significant bit and CF for ROL, or its two highest bits for
ROR. Both source and CL are captured before CL/CH aliases are written. Narrow
parents are preserved; 32-bit results zero the parent, including masked-zero
counts. Reverse dependencies preserve incoming CF/OF when a dynamic count can
be zero; consumers before later kills keep the appropriate earlier definition.
