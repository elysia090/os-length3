# The canon, item by item

`DESIGN.md` held against the books it comes from, each read at the source and taken apart into its own items (the
texts kept as read: Vignelli's *Canon* in full, Müller-Brockmann's *Grid Systems*, Rams's ten principles,
Tschichold's *New Typography*, Gerstner's *Designing Programmes* in part; Bresson's *Notes*, Murch's *In the Blink of
an Eye*, Eisenstein's *Film Form* and *Film Sense*, McCloud's *Understanding Comics*, Richie on Ozu, Tarkovsky's
*Sculpting in Time*; Mascelli's *Five C's* only through quotations checked by others). Every item is one of:

- **kept**: `DESIGN.md` has it, where said;
- **added**: it was missing, and is there now;
- **open**: `DESIGN.md` does more, or other, than the item asks; the person decides (each says what is at stake);
- **not ours**: it is about print, money or a thing this OS does not make.

## Open: what `DESIGN.md` does past its canon

1. **Three type sizes on a sheet** (Vignelli ≤ 2): the name, the words, the small (13), and the numbers' 11.
2. **Half cells** (Müller-Brockmann: whole lines, never halves): a sheet's half line over and under, the frame's
   square a cell and a half.
3. **The head and foot of a piece standing to the foot are one cell each** (Müller-Brockmann: the foot deeper).
4. **A press is heard and seen at once** (Bresson: one preponderant).

Each is a decision of the person's, not a rule to be made true by itself.
